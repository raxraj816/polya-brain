# Invitation draft — not sent

I am preparing PolyaBrain, a technical investigation workbench, for a small private
evaluation. I am looking for engineers who can give candid feedback on a real,
nonconfidential technical problem. Participation is voluntary; negative results
are useful. The product is a prototype and its conclusions require inspection.

For an independent review, please supply a problem and acceptance criteria you
have not used to help build or tune PolyaBrain. We will freeze them before running
two approaches with the same model, evidence/tool access and limits. Please score
the anonymized outputs independently before seeing which approach produced them.

For a pilot session, bring one problem you would otherwise investigate using your
usual tools. A session can stop whenever you want. We will record task outcome,
effort, confusing behavior and any assistance, using a pseudonymous participant ID.
Do not provide credentials, personal data, employer-confidential material or code
you lack permission to share. Recording or sharing any material requires your consent.

Please reply with your role, whether you want to review or participate, and a short
nonconfidential description of a suitable problem. There is no obligation to use
PolyaBrain again or to give positive feedback.
